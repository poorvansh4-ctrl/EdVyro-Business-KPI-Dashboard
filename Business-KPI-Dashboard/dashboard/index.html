Customers in view

This page describes all 15 customers. The filters above do not change it.

Decision question

Which customer groups should a fictional subscription team contact first to reduce avoidable churn? (from the EdVyro Customer Churn Analysis Guide)

The 7 churned customers are exactly the 7 Month-to-Month customers. They are also the customers with 2–14 months' tenure and 3–6 support tickets. With 15 records the data cannot say which trait matters most, and none of these patterns proves cause.

Key findings

  1. Nearly half the customers left. 7 of 15 churned (46.7%). By revenue the loss is 32.3% of MRR, because churned customers paid less per month (₹58.56 vs ₹107.49).
  2. Contract type separates churners completely. Month-to-Month 7 of 7 churned; One Year 0 of 4; Two Year 0 of 4.
  3. Churn is concentrated in the first year. All 6 customers with 12 months' tenure or less churned; 1 of 5 at 13–24 months; 0 of 4 at 25+.
  4. High support contact goes with churn. All 5 customers with 4+ tickets churned; none of the 6 with 0–1. Churned customers averaged 4.3 tickets vs 1.0.
  5. Payment method and plan differences follow the contract mix. Inside each contract type, churn does not vary by payment method or plan.

Revenue risk observations

  • ₹409.93 of ₹1,269.85 monthly revenue (32.3%) is tied to churned customers: about ₹4,919 a year if none are won back (monthly × 12, unchanged prices).
  • 61.0% of revenue at risk is from the Basic plan (₹249.95) and 39.0% from Pro (₹159.98). Enterprise contributes none.
  • Churned customers had paid ₹3,109.51 in lifetime charges, 9.9% of the ₹31,327.18 total, because they left early.
  • Two Year customers hold ₹599.96 (47.2%) of monthly revenue with 0% churn.

Retention opportunities

  • Early-life Month-to-Month customers. Every observed loss happened here, so work aimed at the first 12 months of a Month-to-Month plan covers 100% of revenue at risk.
  • Active watch list. CUST-1006 (One Year, 18 months, Basic, ₹49.99) and CUST-1009 (One Year, 15 months, Pro, ₹79.99) have 2 support tickets each, the most of any active customer. Together: ₹129.98 MRR (15.1% of retained revenue). A descriptive filter, not a prediction.
  • Long contracts are stable. One Year and Two Year customers hold 67.7% of monthly revenue with 0% churn; protect their renewals.

Limitations

  • Small sample. 15 customers; one customer moves a segment's churn rate by 14–50 points.
  • No dates. Churn cannot be shown per month or as a trend.
  • Overlapping traits. Contract, tenure, tickets, plan and age move together.
  • Correlation only. Nothing here shows that changing a contract, plan or payment method changes behaviour.
  • Synthetic data. Fictional training records; no conclusions about real customers.
  • Field names. The data guide lists customer_id, monthly_charge and Monthly / quarterly / annual contracts; the supplied CSV uses CustomerID, MonthlyCharges and Month-to-Month / One Year / Two Year. This dashboard follows the CSV.
